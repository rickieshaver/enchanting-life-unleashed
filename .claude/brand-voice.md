# ELU Brand Voice & Visual Rules

---

## The Brand Personality

**In one sentence:** The wise, witty, high-vibe best friend who's done the shadow work and is living her most aligned life.

She is:
- Empowering, not preachy
- Magical, not performative
- Grounded, not airy
- Direct, not harsh
- Feminine, not dainty
- Luxurious, not extra

She is NOT:
- Toxic positivity ("good vibes only")
- Generic girlboss
- Halloween-costume witchy
- DIY/Pinterest craft energy
- Overly woo or preachy spirituality
- Clip art aesthetics

## Voice & Tone

**Core taglines (use these, don't rewrite them):**
- *"Where Soul Meets Strategy"* — primary tagline
- *"For modern mystics ready to turn their magic into mastery"*
- *"Your power doesn't need permission"*
- *"Aligned. Abundant. Unapologetic."*
- *"You already have the magic. We just help you remember."*

**Always say / key phrases:**
- "aligned," "activated," "magnetic," "expand," "in flow"
- "sacred systems," "energetic boundaries," "nervous system"
- "modern mystic," "soul-led," "intuition meets action"
- "real magic," "no BS," "no fluff," "no toxic positivity"

**Never say:**
- "good vibes only"
- "manifest your dreams" (without grounded action context)
- "hustle harder"
- Overly clinical/corporate language
- Watered-down, wishy-washy language

**Tone by channel:**
| Channel | Tone |
|---------|------|
| Website homepage | Confident, inviting, slightly mysterious |
| Instagram captions | Playful, magical, personal, real |
| Product/sales pages | Empowering, direct, gently persuasive |
| Email | Conversational, encouraging, value-packed |
| Digital products | Warm mentor meets cosmic strategist |

**Style rules:**
- Use contractions ("you're," "it's," "we've") — sounds natural, not stiff
- Strategic emoji use: ✨💫🪄🌙🔮 — never excessive, never mid-sentence spam
- Short punchy sentences mixed with longer flowing ones
- Lead with feelings/transformation, back it up with practical
- No walls of text — breathe, use white space

## Visual Identity

### Color Palette (exact — never approximate)

| Role | Name | Hex | Usage Rule |
|------|------|-----|------------|
| Primary bg | Warm Cream | `#FFFCF7` | 70% of any design — always the base |
| Authority/text | Deep Burgundy | `#6D2E46` | All headers, body text, key elements |
| Accent | Dusty Rose | `#A26769` | Subtitles, decorative elements, borders, writing lines |
| Soft fill | Soft Pink | `#D5B9B2` | Writing boxes, gentle fills, alternating rows |
| Sparkle | Gold | `#EDB74D` | Stars ✦, checkboxes, celebration bullets — USE SPARINGLY |
| Dark bg variant | Plum/Dark | `#3D1A2B` or `#1E0A16` | Dark-mode sections, dramatic hero areas only |
| Accent alt | Cream 2 | `#FBF5F3` | Subtle section alternates |

**Color balance rule: 70% Cream / 20% Burgundy / 10% everything else**

**Common mistakes to avoid:**
- Gold backgrounds or gold text — gold is jewelry only
- All-pink everything — cream is the primary, pink is accent
- Burgundy backgrounds everywhere — use it for text and strategic dark boxes
- Overusing Allura — it's the calligraphy pen, not the workhorse. One use per section max.

### Typography

| Use | Font | Weight | Notes |
|-----|------|--------|-------|
| Page titles, H1 | Playfair Display | Bold 700 | Serif, editorial feel |
| Section headers, H2–H3 | Playfair Display | Medium 500 | |
| Quotes, italics | Playfair Display | Italic | Beautiful italic weight |
| Body text | Poppins | Regular 300–400 | Clean, modern |
| Subheadings, labels | Poppins | SemiBold 600 | |
| Small caps/eyebrows | Poppins | Light 300 | Wide letter-spacing (0.3–0.5em) |
| Accent/decorative | Allura | Regular 400 | Script — taglines, pull quotes, decorative labels, flourishes. USE SPARINGLY. |

### Decorative Language

- **Signature divider:** `■ ✦ ■` (in Dusty Rose)
- **Star accent:** `✦` (in Gold)
- **Moon phases:** 🌑🌒🌓🌔🌕🌖🌗🌘 or use PNG assets in `Images-Elements/`
- **Celestial elements:** subtle, editorial — not Halloween costume
- **White space:** generous — breathing room is a feature, not waste

### Aesthetic Mood Reference

Your brand should evoke:
- Cozy coffee shop with velvet chairs
- Vintage tarot deck meets modern editorial
- Linen-bound journal from a boutique
- Autumn tones, not spring pastels
- Grounded earth, not airy sky
- "Sophisticated witch with a business plan"
